BELIEVERS MANNA TODAY DAILY DEVOTIONAL

Written by Apostle Doctor David A. Aderibigbe

{
Memory Verse

But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.
Matthew 6:33
}

Beloved saint, God is not asking you to chase every need in your own strength. He invites you to seek His kingdom first, and then trust Him with the rest.

{
When your priorities are ordered under Heaven, peace follows. Put prayer before panic. Put worship before worry. Put obedience before opinion.
}

Today, decide again that Jesus is Lord over your schedule, your conversations, and your desires. The Father who clothes the lilies will not abandon His children.

{
Pray with me: Lord, I seek Your kingdom first today. Align my heart with Your will, and teach me to rest in Your faithful provision. Amen.
}
